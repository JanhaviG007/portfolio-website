Wikipedia × ChatGPT Topic Intelligence

Investigated reader navigation after Wikipedia’s ChatGPT article, analysing more than 50,000 clickstream records across 28 articles as part of a team project. Applied TF-IDF, LDA topic modelling and Ridge regression, identifying 7 latent topic clusters (R² = 0.41). Network analysis across 7,343 graph edges identified “Large Language Model” as the most central page.

  • Methods: TF-IDF, LDA, Ridge regression, PageRank and HITS
  • Explored reader interest across AI industry, safety, research and policy themes
  • Presented findings collaboratively as Team HackMates
